Prayer promise

No mother waits long for prayer

When you post a prayer request, trusted Gap Mothers respond so that you are never left carrying it alone. Our goal is a response within 6–12 hours.

Thursday prayer meetings also include testimonies from members, because what God is doing in one home builds faith in the next.
