Community rules

Keeping this space safe

These rules keep the space safe, respectful, and focused.

Confidentiality

What is said here stays here. We protect each other's stories and prayer requests.

Respectful communication

No gossip, criticism, shaming, or aggressive language. We speak with kindness even when we disagree.

God-honoring content

No profanity, political debates, or content that does not align with biblical values.

No unsolicited advice or diagnosis

Share from your experience; don't tell another mom how to parent, medicate, or discipline her son unless asked.

No promotion or spam

No selling products and no self-promotion without permission from the admin.

Admin-led

Admin guides discussions, prayer time, and topics to keep us on mission.

Expectations of members

What we ask of each other

A community only works when we give and receive.

Commit to growth

Come ready to be fed by God's Word and to grow as a disciple and as a mother.

Participate

Engage in prayer, share testimonies, and encourage others. A community only works when we give and receive.

Pray

Pray for the prayer requests shared. Pray for your son(s) and the sons of others in the group.

Be honest and vulnerable

You don't have to be okay. Come as you are and let God and community carry you.

Protect the atmosphere

If you have a concern, bring it to admin privately. Don't create division publicly.

Consistency

We show up, even if it's just to read, pray, and say "Amen."
