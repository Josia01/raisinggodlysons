About us

You don't have to raise your son alone

Raising Godly Sons equips moms of boys through Bible-centered strategies and united prayer, so that motherhood becomes joy.

Our vision

Mothers standing in the gap

We want a community of joyful moms who disciple their sons into Christlike manhood through Scripture and prayer.

Every mother who joins gets the same promise: weekly prayer, Biblical teaching, and a community that holds her up.

"Train up a child in the way he should go; even when he is old he will not depart from it." — Proverbs 22:6

Core values

Six values that shape everything we do

They guide every resource, every gathering, and every prayer we lift together.

Christ-Centered

Jesus is our first model. We raise our sons in Him.

Prayer First

Prayer changes boys and mothers. We commit to praying for our sons and for each other.

Biblical Discipleship

We grow in God's Word together, teaching truth with grace and raising sons with purpose.

Authenticity & Grace

This is a safe place: no perfection, no judgment. Just real moms walking by grace.

Testimony & Hope

We share what God is doing. Our stories build faith for the next mama.

Unity & Encouragement

We are not in competition. We build each other up as we raise the next generation of godly men.

Our first goal

Fifty mothers, deeply engaged

We would rather have 50 mothers who pray, share testimonies, and apply Scripture every week than thousands of passive followers. When we serve 50 well, the systems to reach 500 will already be in place.

Relationships first. Technology second.

Receive weekly prayer, Biblical teaching, and a community that shows up for you.
