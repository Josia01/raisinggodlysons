How we grow

A four-phase ministry roadmap

Rather than building everything at once, we grow with intention: depth of relationship over breadth of reach, and sustainable rhythms over rapid growth.

Weeks 1–2

Build your foundation

Establish the core ministry promise, create accounts across all platforms, and launch a focused five-page website that converts visitors into community members.

  • Define the core offer and weekly schedule
  • Create all ministry platform accounts
  • Launch five-page ministry website

Weeks 3–4

Build community systems

Structure the WhatsApp community with dedicated groups, establish a predictable weekly ministry rhythm, and create the first ten downloadable resources.

  • Launch structured WhatsApp Community
  • Establish weekly posting rhythm
  • Prepare first ten resource downloads

Months 2–3

Begin content production

Use the One Content, Many Formats method to multiply reach from a single weekly teaching. Launch the first campaign: 30 Days of Praying for Your Son.

  • One teaching → multiple platform formats
  • Launch first 30-day prayer campaign
  • Grow email newsletter subscribers

Months 3–6

Create leadership systems

Recruit trusted Gap Mothers as community leaders. Formalise a prayer response system so no mother waits more than 6–12 hours for prayer support.

  • Appoint and equip Gap Mothers
  • Operationalise 6–12hr prayer response
  • Scale community with proven systems
