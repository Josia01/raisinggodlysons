What every mother receives

Prayer

Intercession for you and your son, with real people who respond when you ask.

Always

How it works

Joining takes three steps

No experience needed and no perfection required.

Step 1

Sign up

Tell us your name and the age of your son(s) so we can point you to the right resources.

Step 2

Join on WhatsApp

We add you to the community groups: announcements, prayer requests, discussion and more.

Step 3

Pray and grow

Take part in the weekly rhythm, share your needs, and encourage other mothers.

"To have a community of joyful moms standing in the gap, discipling their sons into Christlike manhood through Scripture and prayer."
