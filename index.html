Founder's story

A vision placed on a mother's heart

This vision was placed on my heart by God. As a mother of sons, I understand the weight and the beauty of raising them on my knees. I have witnessed God's grace sustain me in this calling.

It is for this reason I started Raising Godly Sons: a ministry for mothers like you who believe that our sons are called to greatness and to Godly purpose. Welcome, Mama.

What we hold dear

Our core values

Six commitments shape everything we do together.

Christ-centered

Jesus is our first model; we raise our sons in Him.

Prayer first

We believe prayer changes boys and mothers. We commit to praying for our sons and for each other.

Biblical discipleship

We grow in God's Word together, teach truth with grace, and raise sons with purpose.

Authenticity and grace

A safe place with no perfection and no judgment. Just real moms walking by grace.

Testimony and hope

We share what God is doing. Our stories build faith for the next Mama.

Unity and encouragement

We are not in competition; we build each other up as we raise the next generation of godly men.
