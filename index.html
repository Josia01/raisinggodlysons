About MGL

A family enterprise that stayed close to the whole chain.

Mitekaro Group Limited began in Iringa, Tanzania, and grew division by division — each one built to remove a step between our land and our buyers.

FIELD

Agriculture & forestry — Maize, rice, and beans grown and sourced across Iringa; pine and eucalyptus raised on MGL-managed plantations for timber export.

DESK

Vuna Marketplace — Our technology division, giving farmers and buyers a direct digital trading floor at vunamarketplace.com.

ROAD

Fleet & logistics — Our own trucks carry grain, beans, and timber from farm and forest to port, and haul freight for outside clients too.

PORT

Export desk — Cargo is graded, documented, and shipped to regional and international buyers under the MGL name.
