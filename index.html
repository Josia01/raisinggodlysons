Who We Are Christ-Centered · Prayer First · Discipleship

Our Core Values

Raising Godly Sons equips moms of boys through Bible-centered strategies and united prayer to raise godly men — building a community where motherhood becomes joy.

Six core values shape every resource, every gathering, and every prayer we lift together.

  • Christ-Centered — Jesus is our first model; we raise our sons in Him.
  • Prayer First — We believe prayer changes boys and mothers. We commit to praying for our sons and for each other.
  • Biblical Discipleship — We grow in God's Word together, teaching truth with grace and raising sons with purpose.
  • Authenticity & Grace — This is a safe place — no perfection, no judgment. Just real moms walking by grace.
  • Testimony & Hope — We share what God is doing. Our stories build faith for the next Mama.
  • Unity & Encouragement — We are not in competition; we build each other up as we raise the next generation of godly men.
How We Grow

A Four-Phase Ministry Roadmap

Rather than building everything at once, we grow with intention — prioritising depth of relationship over breadth of reach, and sustainable rhythms over rapid growth.

1

Weeks 1–2

Build Your Foundation

Establish the core ministry promise, create accounts across all platforms, and launch a focused five-page website that converts visitors into community members.

  • Define the core offer and weekly schedule
  • Create all ministry platform accounts
  • Launch five-page ministry website
2

Weeks 3–4

Build Community Systems

Structure the WhatsApp community with dedicated groups, establish a predictable weekly ministry rhythm, and create the first ten downloadable resources.

  • Launch structured WhatsApp Community
  • Establish weekly posting rhythm
  • Prepare first ten resource downloads
3

Months 2–3

Begin Content Production

Use the One Content, Many Formats method to multiply reach from a single weekly teaching. Launch the first campaign: 30 Days of Praying for Your Son.

  • One teaching → multiple platform formats
  • Launch first 30-day prayer campaign
  • Grow email newsletter subscribers
4

Months 3–6

Create Leadership Systems

Recruit trusted Gap Mothers as community leaders. Formalise a prayer response system so no mother waits more than 6–12 hours for prayer support.

  • Appoint and equip Gap Mothers
  • Operationalise 6–12hr prayer response
  • Scale community with proven systems

Our First Goal
50

Deeply Engaged Mothers

"Aim for 50 deeply engaged mothers who participate consistently each week — not 30,000 passive followers."

Fifty well-supported, active mothers who pray together, share testimonies, and apply Biblical teaching will grow this ministry far more powerfully than thousands of passive observers. When we serve 50 well, the systems to reach 500 will already be in place.

This ministry is community-centred. Relationships first. Technology second.
